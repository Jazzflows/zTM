# LinkedIn Writing Style

Applies to the Zoth company page and to founder posts on LinkedIn. Every rule in house-style also applies.

## 1. The spec (as set by the user, 31 October 2026)

> Write like an analyst, not a marketer. Line 1 is a fact, number, or problem under 15 words. Every claim needs a number or named entity. Explain why a problem exists before the fix. 80 to 200 words, 1 to 2 sentence paragraphs, → for lists, one CTA last as "Action: link". No emoji, no hashtags, no dashes as punctuation. Never use: thrilled, excited, seamless, game changer, empower, revolutionize, journey. Say USDC not USD; payment financing not private credit. Never put Zocta Points on the same line as an APY. zMAG7 is equity, not yield, and needs "Available to non-US persons in eligible jurisdictions." Never invent figures; write [FIGURE NEEDED]. Give FLAGS, 3 hooks, then the post.

## 2. How to apply it

**Shape of every post**

| Block | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Line 1 | One fact, number or problem, under 15 words. It must work alone, because LinkedIn truncates after the first lines |
| Why | One or two short paragraphs on why the problem exists: the mechanism, the cost, the named actor |
| Fix | The Zoth product as the answer, stated through what it does, with a number or named entity |
| List (optional) | 3 to 4 lines, each starting with →, each a concrete fact |
| Separate lines | Zocta Points (if relevant) on a line of their own; the eligibility line on any zMAG7 post; the yield disclaimer on any yield post |
| Last line | One CTA, written exactly as "Action: link" |

**Rules that sit behind the spec**

- Length: 80 to 200 words, counted on the post body. Paragraphs of 1 to 2 sentences.
- "Every claim needs a number or named entity": a named partner, regulator, market, product or registry counts. A general adjective never does.
- "No dashes as punctuation": no em dashes, no en dashes, no spaced hyphens. Hyphens inside compound words (full-stack, non-US, short-term) are fine.
- "Say USDC not USD": USDC for anything deposited, held or paid out. USD appears only when it names a currency hedge or a fiat price, and even then rephrase where possible.
- "Never put Zocta Points on the same line as an APY": Zocta Points get their own sentence on their own line. No "16% APY", no "12% + 4%".
- "zMAG7 is equity, not yield": describe it in the positive ("zMAG7 is equity exposure. Its value moves with the seven stocks it holds.") and close with "Available to non-US persons in eligible jurisdictions." Never frame it as bypassing brokers, banks or borders.
- "Never invent figures": every figure comes from fact-sheet, community-sale or the user's message. Missing figure: [FIGURE NEEDED: what]. Volatile figure: [CHECK].
- The house banned list extends the spec's list: also unlock, cutting-edge, democratize, next-gen, revolutionary, "the future of finance".
- No negative framing and no negative parallelism in the post itself. The spec's "analyst, not a marketer" is an instruction to the writer, not a pattern for the copy.
- Sources: put the source link for every figure in FLAGS, so the reviewer can check it. The post itself carries only the one Action link.
- Community sale posts: "community sale", never "IDO"; no TGE date or countdown; cashback framed as 10% to the participant and 5% to the referrer.

**Founder posts (Pritam Dutta)**

Same spec, first person. Every founder post leads with a product that is already live and keeps Zoth in a positive light. Finished copy with no slots for the founder to fill. No negative, self-critical or hostile openings. Line 1 is still a fact, number or problem under 15 words.

## 3. Output format

Every LinkedIn request returns, in this order:

1. **FLAGS**: each [CHECK], [FIGURE NEEDED] and [VERIFY] item, the source for each figure used, and any compliance line still in draft. "FLAGS: none" if clean.
2. **3 hooks**: three alternative first lines, each under 15 words, each built on a different angle (a number, a problem, a mechanism).
3. **The post**: written on the strongest hook, 80 to 200 words, ending "Action: link".

## 4. Worked example

**FLAGS**
- 12% base APY [CHECK]: 12.10% as of 3 September 2026 (fact-sheet, zOPAL).
- [FIGURE NEEDED]: the standard settlement window for Brazilian credit card sales, with a source link.
- Yield disclaimer is a draft line; approved wording is still open (open-items 12).

**3 hooks**
1. zOPAL targets 12% base APY in USDC from one source: Brazilian card receivables.
2. Every card sale in Brazil creates a receivable someone has to finance.
3. Brazilian card sales settle on a delay, and that delay is the yield.

**Post**

zOPAL targets 12% base APY in USDC from one source: short-term Brazilian card receivables.

The yield comes from a timing gap. Brazilian merchants sell on card today and receive the cash [FIGURE NEEDED: standard settlement window] later.

Merchants who want that cash sooner sell the receivable at a discount. Financing that gap is payment financing, and the discount is the yield.

zOPAL is an institutional-grade yield vault that gives USDC holders access to that cash flow:
→ Receivables registered under BCB Resolution 522
→ Brazilian real exposure hedged
→ Daily liquidity, no lock-up
→ Every transfer and redemption screened against sanctions and blocked-address lists

Zocta Points accrue separately on every deposit.

Target yield, variable and not guaranteed. Capital is at risk.

Action: app.zoth.io/zvault

## 5. Pre-post checklist

1. Is line 1 a fact, number or problem under 15 words?
2. Does the post explain why the problem exists before the fix?
3. Does every claim carry a number or named entity?
4. Is the body 80 to 200 words, in 1 to 2 sentence paragraphs?
5. Are Zocta Points on their own line, away from any APY?
6. Does any zMAG7 mention close with the eligibility line?
7. No emoji, no hashtags, no dashes as punctuation, no banned words?
8. Is there exactly one CTA, last, as "Action: link"?
9. Are FLAGS complete, with a source for every figure?
