# Zoth House Style (zTM)

Every Zoth-branded piece follows this file. Channel files (x-style, linkedin-style) add to it and never relax it. Where an older rule and a newer rule conflict, the newer rule is the one written here; the history sits in workstreams-and-decisions.

## 1. Positioning and boilerplate

- Company line: "The full-stack stablecoin neobank for the Global South and the agentic economy." Use it for bios, footers and brand kits.
- Long boilerplate: "Zoth is building a full-stack stablecoin neobank for the Global South and the emerging Agentic Economy, an integrated financial infrastructure that unifies yield, payments, cards, liquidity, compliance, and security into a single, institution-grade platform for individuals, institutions, and AI agents."
- Never use privacy-first, privacy-preserving, privacy rails, privacy-focused or "Private Vault" as company positioning.
- "Global South" is always capitalised.
- zVaults positioning: zMAG7 is part of zVaults and is built on Coinbase's tokenized stock infrastructure. zVaults runs permissioned and permissionless vaults on a foundational fund structure; the permissioned side uses a CIMA-regulated structure governed in the BVI, built for large institutional liquidity. Never say every Zoth yield product sits inside one framework.

## 2. Product claims

### zOPAL
- Always an "institutional-grade yield vault".
- "zOPAL targets 12% base APY in USDC." A standalone 12% is correct and intentional: it is the base-yield thesis.
- The yield comes from payment financing of short-term Brazilian credit card receivables. Never "private credit" or "credit strategy". Real cash flows, daily liquidity, no lock-up.
- Zocta Points go in their own sentence and, on social, their own line. Never "16% APY", never "12% + 4%" on one line, never a points figure added into a yield figure.
- zOPAL is not built on FAAST. Never disclose its credit rating.
- Strategy AUM and vault TVL are different numbers; never conflate them.

### zMAG7
- zMAG7 is equity exposure. No yield, APY, return, income or passive income language, ever. Its value moves with the seven stocks and can fall.
- Every tokenized stock post carries: "Available to non-US persons in eligible jurisdictions."
- Approved claim: "Every tokenized stock zMAG7 holds is backed by a real listed share." Never write that zMAG7 or its token gives a claim on a real share. A generic description of what a tokenized stock is may say "a claim on a real share held by a third-party custodian".
- Never frame zMAG7 as bypassing brokers, banks or borders. Never write "no US bank account", "no broker", "no minimum" or similar access claims. Make the point through what the product is: access to the companies driving global growth, an equal-weight basket, minted with USDC from $1, rebalanced every 3 days.
- Two ad tracks that never cross: underserved markets get the access-to-growth story (within the rail above); already-served and crypto-native markets (Korea included) get the basket-versus-single-stock and Zocta angles. Waitlist ads and launch ads are separate campaigns with separate copy.
- Quality benchmark: match the standard of the established tokenized stock players.

### Community sale and $ZOTH
- "Zoth Community Sale" or "community sale". Never "IDO" in external copy.
- Never state the TGE date in body copy, tables, titles, meta descriptions, subject lines or captions. No countdowns to it. Other announced dates stay as calendar dates.
- Cashback framing: "15% cash back to you and your referral" means 10% plus 5% across two people. Never imply 15% to one person.
- $ZOTH utility: use the offering page wording only. Zoth runs no L1 or L2, so never imply chain-fee utility.
- IDO-era copy references for quality: Tria, Multipli, Plasma and Plume.

## 4. Compliance and disclosure

- Every public yield claim carries a yield disclaimer. The approved wording is still open (open-items 12); until then use a short draft line and flag it.
- Never write guaranteed, risk-free, safe, passive income or any promise of returns.
- Never invent a figure. Write [FIGURE NEEDED] and list it in FLAGS. Tag volatile figures [CHECK].
- Never quote Strategy AUM, TVL or TPV in one-to-one outreach copy (those figures move).
- Security copy stays high-level and outcome-framed: multi-layer security, audited operational security, policy-level access management, endpoint security, expert-managed cybersecurity. Never name the vendors who did the work, never expose operational specifics (durations, addresses, runbooks, personnel, signer counts).
- Never make a direct licence claim beyond the approved CIMA and BVI structure wording.
- Never name non-partner competitors (Aave, Morpho, Aerodrome, Wasabi, Beefy, Superform and similar) in public copy without approval. Partners may be named; everyone else is "protocols" or "the ecosystem".
- Never degrade a partner, including hypothetical failure framing ("if [partner] failed"). State protections positively: segregated, bankruptcy-remote, held outside the balance sheet.
- Never mention Movement or Neemo.

## 5. Voice

- Analyst register: claim first, then proof. Every claim carries a number, a named entity or a concrete mechanism.
- Write with conviction. Lead with the claim, state figures plainly, no stacked hedges. Raise a genuine conflict once, in its own place.
- Active voice, short direct sentences, no throat-clearing. Long-form follows the fluid style of crypto KOL writing (reference: Stacy Muur) for an institutional, crypto-savvy reader: depth, not explainer framing.
- No negative framing ("not dead", "not gone") and no negative parallelism ("not X, it is Y", "rather than left to drift", "not just X, but Y"). Write the positive form.
- No stacks of single 5 to 6 word lines, one per idea. Each sentence carries value.
- Third person in branded documents; no "we" or "our" in formal documents. No contractions in formal copy. Founder posts use first person and spoken voice.
- Hyperlink facts, figures and studies to their source wherever the format allows. In social posts, sources go in FLAGS.
- Calendar dates only. Never "today", "tomorrow", "yesterday", "this week".

## 6. Banned language

Words: thrilled, excited, seamless, game changer, game-changing, empower, revolutionize, revolutionary, journey, unlock, cutting-edge, democratize, next-gen, "the future of finance", "ecosystem" as filler, LFG, Based.

Punctuation: no em dashes and no en dashes, anywhere. Use full stops, commas, colons or →. Hyphens inside compound words (full-stack, non-US) are fine.

AI tells: "Here's the thing", "Let that sink in", "Let's dive in", "In a world where", "Imagine a world", a rhetorical question answered in one word ("The result? Freedom."), three adjectives in a row, a closing line that restates the opening, "No X, no Y, no Z" triads.

Retired phrases: "That era is ending", "Coming soon." or "Opening soon." as a final line, "Institutional-grade" more than once per piece.

## 7. Channel formats

### Telegram blasts (28 Sep 2026 format)
Short. Open with "gm" and the group tags. One line of news ("Big news from our side"). One claim line naming the product. One dense sentence on what it does. The live or launch status with the product link. Then an amplification ask pointing at the X announcement post (boost and engage). A separate "Hey team" variant goes to leadership and partner teams asking for launch amplification.

### Press releases
- PR and social copy are separate pieces, written and cleared separately. A release is never reused as the X post, and the reverse.
- Wire dateline: uppercase city, date, wire tag, Dubai only: "DUBAI, Oct. 8, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE)". The dateline city stays the same across translations; only the date format localises.
- Releases published to the zoth.io blog carry no dateline.

### Blogs and long-form
Claims-first structure, table-forward where data is compared. Launch long-form follows the manager's order: the announcement, then the pain, then the emerging-market claims, then the explainer (what the product is and how it solves the pain), then what Zoth is building. No negative parallelism.

### Strategy, plans and checklists
State the plan, not the rationale. Prefer a visual grid or flow. Launch checklists bundle activities by phase (before launch, launch day, after launch) with a few points per workstream and no numbered sub-tasks. A blockers list holds only genuine open questions (budgets, signatures, compliance positions, build dates).

### Outreach
Copy-and-send messages, one per named person, with the user's own note beside each draft, a LinkedIn link and, where possible, an email address. Outgoing emails describe the relationship, never the state of a CRM record.

### Visuals
Zoth dark theme: near-black ground, orange accents (#FF4509 for headers and table headers with white text). No kicker or footer strip on zVaults or zPayments social images. Partner sections carry real logos.
