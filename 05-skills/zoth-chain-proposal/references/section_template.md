# Section Template, Content Slots

This is the canonical structure. Each section shows the slots to fill and the proven phrasing patterns. Use the Avalanche concise version (8 pages) as the structural baseline.

## Section 1, Title and Executive Summary (Page 1)

**Title:** `Zoth × [Chain]`

**Subtitle:** `The Stablecoin Neobank for [Chain]`

**Tagline (italic, dark text):** Mention the products + the special angle, comma-separated.
- Default: "Yield Infrastructure, Tokenized US Equities, Cross-Border Payments, Cards & Privacy-Enabled Stack"
- Themed example: "Yield Infrastructure, AI & Tech US Tokenized Equities for SEA, Cross-Border Payments, Cards & Privacy-Enabled Stack"

**Para 1:** "Zoth is a stablecoin-first neobank, the financial operating system for on-chain economies. Deploying the full Zoth stack on [Chain] brings [products], and a 1.8M+ wallet distribution network natively to [Chain] [terminology like C-Chain]."

**Para 2 (two-phase build):** "Two-phase build. Phase 1: zPayments and zVault yield infrastructure go live natively on [Chain] [terminology], $25M+ monthly TPV, up to [X]% RWA-backed APY from Day 1. Phase 2: the first fully integrated [Chain]-native neobank under a co-branded identity, [products listed] unified in one app, one wallet, one KYC, one balance."

**Strength × What [Chain] Gets table** (7 rows typical):
- zVault Liquid Assets, RWA-backed, live
- zStocks: [generic or themed]
- zPayments cross-border settlement
- Zocta Card, the spend layer
- 1.8M+ wallet user base
- Privacy-Enabled Infrastructure (joint exploration)
- Zocta Rewards, on-chain loyalty (optional)

**4-metric strip:** Sticky TVL | MAU | Settlement Volume | Active zVaults

**The Proposition:** Bold orange label, then "anchor + grant + chain incentive co-funding + co-marketing" sentence.
