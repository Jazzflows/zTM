## Cashback and referral

Participate in the Community Sale and receive 10% cashback in zMAG7. Refer someone who participates and receive 5% cashback in zMAG7; the referred participant still earns their own 10%. Outreach framing: "15% cash back to you and your referral" (10% plus 5% across two people, never 15% to one person).

zMAG7 cashback goes to eligible non-US participants. The page FAQ says US participants receive rewards in USDC and USDT (see open-items: this conflicts with the Regulation S disclaimer).

The referral link is generated after connecting a wallet. Referral codes use letters and numbers only. The dashboard tracks successful referrals and total referred contributions in USDC.

The referral program follows the Inner Circle one-pager mechanics: purchase cashback plus referrer cashback, both paid in zMAG7. Referral is emphasised heavily across all sale content.

## How the sale is being marketed

The single KPI is retail deposits into the sale. Zoth has roughly 300 to 400 funded users across its products, so the existing base cannot fill the sale alone.

Content: all sale content (PR, social, blogs, graphic placeholders) is planned in the Google Doc "Zoth Community Sale (Master Doc)" (https://docs.google.com/document/d/194ZQKmIhEQuxAWMBos9xzlfjS9qZnMiu3TKVXFk7pxg). It runs on a T-minus and T-plus mechanic where T is the sale open (16 November 2026) and the whitelist opens around T-14. Layout: week-wise top-level tabs with dated activity sub-tabs, plus a PR Announcement tab with separate PR copy and social copy sub-tabs.

Tracking: a separate Google Sheet for the marketing head and the founder to skim, with week groups, day rows and one sub-row per item, carrying status, owner and go-live date only (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1dvVwiw0U0ijXy3YGpYE1o_7aiHDDY0l0ioqu2ZNouIc).

Cadence: 3 to 4 founder posts and 4 to 5 Zoctopus posts a week, inside a total capacity of 4 to 5 posts a day across all channels.

Distribution: Mindo AI is the exclusive KOL partner, with campaigns aimed at bringing people into the sale (investors get a percentage back in zMAG7). Direct outreach targets communities, group owners, alpha groups, traders and farming collectives at 20 to 25 direct messages a day, one market lane per day. DeBank outreach uses @zoctopus7 as the Telegram contact on purpose. Regional activation runs on Telegram and X, channel by channel. An agency and influencer push runs from late October 2026 for three to six months.

Founder direction: the TGE narrative is "you missed the community sale, here is your chance to get ZOTH at TGE", and the founder expects the sale to be oversubscribed. Zoctopus rides alongside the bigger idea as a continuity signature (about 1.5k followers), not as the spine of the plan.

Launchpad: SONAR (Coinbase). SONAR's own whitelist mechanics, KYC and integration timeline govern the dates.
