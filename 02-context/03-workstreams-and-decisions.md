# zTM Workstreams and Decision Log

Status as of 31 October 2026. Each workstream lists what it is, where it stands and the rules attached to it. The decision log at the end records every dated change in order, so a later rule can be traced back to what it replaced.

## Active workstreams

### 1. Zoth Community Sale (the main push)
The $ZOTH token sale on Coinbase SONAR. Whitelist opens 2 November 2026, sale runs 16 to 29 November 2026, allocation 5 December 2026, TGE date withheld. Every channel, campaign and partner now feeds this. Full detail in community-sale.

### 2. Founder-led marketing (FLM)
Posts for the founder, Pritam Dutta, on X and LinkedIn, at 3 to 4 a week during the sale push. The working direction (23 October 2026) is finished, shippable copy that keeps Zoth in a positive light and builds the Zoth story, every post leading with a product that is already live. No placeholder slots for the founder to fill. No negative, self-critical or hostile openings. The earlier storytelling standard the founder shared (a Tim Denning article: open on a surprise, state the feeling, spoken voice with contractions, short, no hero's journey, no pandering) still informs voice, but the 23 October direction governs.

### 3. Zoctopus
The official mascot and "finance buddy", on a Pudgy Penguins style model (partners, merch, offline events). 4 to 5 posts a week during the sale push. Treated as a continuity signature asset, not a launch driver. Voice variant in x-style.

### 4. Referral and outreach
The referral program pays 10% cashback in zMAG7 to participants and 5% to referrers. Mindo AI is the exclusive KOL partner. The user runs a direct outreach push to communities, group owners, alpha groups, traders and farming collectives (20 to 25 DMs a day, one market lane per day). Outreach output is copy-and-send messages, one per named person, with the user's note on that person beside the draft, plus a LinkedIn link and, where possible, an email address for every named person.

### 5. Regional community channels
Restructuring the Telegram supergroup ("Zoth Ecosystem") and the Discord server (which already has country channels) into regional and language rooms aligned to the launch markets, starting with a limited region set. Execution sits with the community manager on the user's team.

### 6. Launch markets
Broad set (24 September 2026): Korea, China via Hong Kong, Japan, LATAM, Brazil, Spain, France, Indonesia. The exact final set is not confirmed.

### 7. MAGNET to Zocta conversion
An announced campaign converting legacy MAGNET points into Zocta Points. Holders raise a Discord ticket with wallet address and Magnets held; the community manager logs them in MAGNET_to_Zocta_Tracker.xlsx (conversion log tab and how-to-fill tab).

### 8. zMAG7 B2B distribution
Outreach to wallets, neobanks, fintechs and banks to integrate zMAG7 into their own earn layer. Separate from the retail and community push.

### 9. Korea partnership (SOOHO.IO)
SOOHO.IO introduces zMAG7 to its Korean retail base and connections. Agreed in principle, nothing signed as of September 2026, no technical integration, no payments scope. Korea is an already-served market, so it gets the basket-versus-single-stock story, never the access story. Partner research in 04-knowledge-docs/claude/sooho-io-partner-context.md.

### 10. zVaults app copy and mobile redesign
Copy for the redesigned app.zoth.io: landing, zOPAL and zMAG7 vault pages, Portfolio, Zocta, referral gate, deposit modal. Pages marked "old" keep the live copy exactly; pages marked "new" get copy written from scratch, including tooltip text. Docs tab links (Factsheet, Terms and Conditions, Documentation) point to docs.zoth.io and stay as placeholders until added. The user is also redesigning the zVaults mobile UX in the Figma "Mobile design" file. Problems to fix: vaults visible only after scrolling, Deposit and Withdraw buttons lost on scroll, two steps to switch between zOPAL and zMAG7, a hard-to-read horizontal strategy table, the Zocta banner placed too low. The zOPAL page rework keeps the live design, cuts redundant content and re-sorts sections, with Lombard's LBTC app page as the reference.

### 11. Domain rating and PR
Raising zoth.io's domain rating and building PR capability. The blog lives on-domain at zoth.io/blog. No paid SEO tool (no Ahrefs, no Semrush). The 2025 exploit narrative is already handled and out of scope. PR and social copy are always written and cleared as separate pieces.

### 12. Agency and influencer push
Several marketing agencies with influencers onboard from late October 2026 for three to six months, expected to drive heavy traffic to the website and zPayments pages. Public content (website, GitBook) needs to be free of wrong information and typos before the traffic lands.

### 13. Meme coin partnership
Of three angles mapped on 29 September 2026, the decision is angle 2: partner with an existing top meme coin. Launching Zoth's own meme coin is rejected. Launching the yield product on Robinhood is possible but needs more effort, may displease Base, and has no timing.
