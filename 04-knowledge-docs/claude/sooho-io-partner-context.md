# SOOHO.IO: partner context (Korea)

Status as of 23 September 2026: partnership with Zoth agreed in principle, no signed document. **Scope: bringing zEQTY to the Korean market only. It is NOT a payments partnership. Keep zPayments and stablecoin payment rails out of all collateral for it.**

## Who they are
- **Name:** SOOHO.IO Inc. (수호아이오). Often mistyped as "Sohoo" or "Suho IO". Website sooho.io.
- **What:** Seoul blockchain financial infrastructure company building for regulated institutions since 2019. Not a neobank and not a consumer app.
- **CEO:** Jisu Park (Park Ji-soo). HQ: B1, 126 Teheran-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul.
- **Credentials:** first Korean company backed by ConsenSys; named to the FSC "K-Fintech 30"; worked on the Bank of Korea CBDC and purpose-bound money (PBM) programme; safeguards more than $2.4B in assets ([Financial News, 4 Feb 2026](https://en.fnnews.com/news/202602041557352945)).

## Market wedge
SOOHO.IO sits on the won side, between Korean financial institutions and global stablecoin and tokenized-asset networks. It wins on regulatory credibility (Bank of Korea and FSC work) and on its network of global rails (Chainlink, Partior, AllUnity, 21X). It sells infrastructure to institutions, not accounts to consumers.

## Why it fits Zoth (zEQTY scope)
- **Distribution:** an institutional route to Korean investors, who hold close to $200B in US shares.
- **Funding leg:** KRW FX and settlement for zEQTY deposits through Ezys.
- **Launch stage:** SDMS as a Seoul venue for the partnership.
- **Shared partner:** both companies work with Chainlink.

## Copy conventions for Korea PR
- In English copy, write amounts as the number, then lowercase "won", with US dollars in brackets: "7 trillion won (about $4.8 billion)". Use "KRW" in FX and stablecoin contexts, and "Korean won (KRW)" on first descriptive mention. Do not use the ₩ symbol in news copy.

## Korea regulatory context (verify before use)
- In July 2026 the FSC classified tokenized stocks as securities under the Capital Markets Act ([Bloomingbit](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/115975)). Retail marketing in Korea needs a licensed intermediary and counsel review.
- Tokenized securities roadmap announced 4 Sep 2026. Phase one starts February 2027 ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/09/04/south-korea-targets-february-2027-rollout-for-full-tokenized-securities-market)).
- The Digital Asset Basic Act (which covers KRW stablecoin issuance) had not passed as of September 2026. Do not claim that a KRW stablecoin on-ramp is live.
- Korea is an already-served market for US equities. Under the zEQTY two-track rule it gets the basket-versus-single-stock story, not the access story.
